# Task Tracking

![task-tracking](assets/images/en/task-tracking.png)

> The practice of maintaining an explicit, visible list of a task's steps with their status, kept up to date as an agent works through them. Where a scratchbook holds the agent's private reasoning, task tracking externalizes progress: a human can see at a glance what is done, what is in flight, and what remains, and can interrupt or redirect before effort is wasted. It also disciplines the agent itself, since steps that were decomposed and written down are less likely to be silently dropped during a long session.
